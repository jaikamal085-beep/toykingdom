Returns & Refund Policy

Simple, transparent and fair: here's how returns work at Toy Kingdom.

At Toy Kingdom, we work hard to ensure that every customer receives high-quality, safely packed products. We understand that issues can sometimes occur during transit, and we want to make the return process simple, transparent, and fair. To maintain the quality of our service and to protect our customers as well as our business, we follow a strict return policy that ensures genuine claims are handled smoothly.

What can be returned

Returns are accepted only for products that are damaged or defective at the time of delivery.

360° unboxing video is mandatory

To verify the issue and process your request quickly, a 360° unboxing video is mandatory. The video must clearly show:

  • the package from all sides before opening,
  • the full unsealing process, and
  • the condition of the product immediately upon removal from the box.

Please record in good lighting, showing the product, outer packaging, labels and any visible damage or defect. This helps us confirm the issue and avoid misuse of the return policy.

Send it within 24 hours

Send the unedited 360° unboxing video to our official WhatsApp number 82486 40441 within 24 hours of receiving the order. Requests sent after 24 hours, or without proper video proof, will not be eligible for return.

What happens next

Once we receive your video, our team will carefully review it and verify the issue. If the claim is approved, we will guide you through the next steps, such as arranging a product pickup, issuing a replacement (subject to stock availability), or processing a refund as per our policy.

Not eligible for return

  • Products damaged due to customer handling
  • Orders with missing packaging
  • Any reason other than manufacturing defects or transit damage

We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in helping us maintain a smooth and trustworthy return process.

Need to report a problem?

  1. Record a 360° unboxing video before opening
  2. Send it on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery
  3. We review and arrange a pickup, replacement or refund
